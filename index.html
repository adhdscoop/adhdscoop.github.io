New ADHD medication centanafadine: what four trials found

A review in the journal PCN Reports looked at all four trials that compared the new ADHD medication centanafadine with a placebo, a pill with no medicine in it. About 1,800 kids, teens and adults took part, and each trial lasted 6 weeks. In the trial of kids aged 6 to 12, about 34 out of every 100 kids on either dose had a big drop in ADHD symptoms, against about 23 out of every 100 kids on the placebo. About 39 out of every 100 kids on the higher dose reported an adverse health event, meaning any health problem during the trial, whether or not the medication caused it. On the placebo it was about 25 out of every 100. Nothing here covers use past 6 weeks, no trial compared the drug with an older ADHD medication, and all four trials appear to have been sponsored by the company developing the drug.

Gul et al., Psychiatry and Clinical Neurosciences Reports (2026). "Dose-dependent efficacy and safety of centanafadine in patients with attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder: A systematic review of the latest evidence."

November 17, 2026

Adults with ADHD and the connection to smoking

Researchers used health data from UK adults aged 40 and older in a large project called UK Biobank. They compared 133 adults who said a doctor had diagnosed them with ADHD with 1,330 similar adults with no ADHD diagnosis. About 60 out of every 100 adults with an ADHD diagnosis had smoked at some point in their lives. Among adults with no ADHD diagnosis, it was about 43 out of every 100. In this small study the link showed up in men and not in women, and most people who had smoked had quit. The study shows a link. It does not show that one causes the other.

Kozin Porat, Aronis & Weinstein, BMC Psychiatry (2026).

November 16, 2026

ADHD and bullying: are kids with ADHD bullied more than other kids?

A researcher at Shinshu University in Japan surveyed the parents of 1,594 kids aged 6 to 18. Half of the parents said their child had been diagnosed with ADHD by a health professional, and half said their child had not. In the past two months, parents said 31 out of every 100 kids with ADHD had been bullied. For kids without ADHD, it was about 8 out of every 100. Most kids with ADHD, 69 out of every 100, had not been bullied in that time. The gap was largest in elementary school. The study is one survey of parents at one point in time. It shows a link. It does not show that one causes the other.

Takahashi, Frontiers in Psychiatry (2026). "ADHD-related mental health and school impairments across educational stages: sex-specific patterns in Japanese youth."

November 15, 2026

ADHD symptoms, reading and math

Scientists in Finland followed about 2,000 kids from grade 1 to grade 9. When a kid's ADHD symptoms rose above their own usual level, a later dip in understanding what they read tended to follow. The study found no link to later reading speed or math. It measured symptoms on a questionnaire, not diagnoses. The study shows a pattern over time. It does not show that one causes the other.

Psyridou et al., Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry (2026). "ADHD symptoms and learning difficulties: co-occurrence and directionality from Grades 1 to 9."

November 13, 2026

Attention and brain sync in kids

Scientists looked at brain scans of 9- and 10-year-olds from a large US study, with and without ADHD. They checked how in sync different brain areas were, and how well each child could ignore distractions on a test. Attention was tied to brain sync, and so was ADHD. The tie between attention and brain sync was about the same in both groups. The study shows these things go together. It does not show that one causes the other.

Harkness et al., Journal of Attention Disorders (2026). "The Relationship Between Inhibitory Control of Attention and fMRI Functional Connectivity in Children With and Without ADHD."

November 12, 2026

ADHD after 50 and dementia risk

Scientists used health records from 187,341 US adults aged 50 and older. About 7 in 100 adults with ADHD later had dementia or milder memory problems. For adults without ADHD, it was about 3 in 100. Most adults with ADHD never did. The study shows the two go together. It does not show that one causes the other.

Bauer-Negrini et al., JAMA Psychiatry (2026). "Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder and Late-Life Cognitive Impairment."

3 brain patterns in kids with ADHD and anxiety

Scientists looked at brain scans from 3,508 kids with ADHD, anxiety, or both. They found three brain patterns. Each pattern came with its own mix of symptoms. The patterns are a research finding. They are not a test anyone can take.

Nature Mental Health (2026). "Deep learning of brain-behavior dimensions identifies transdiagnostic biotypes in youth with ADHD and anxiety disorders."

November 12, 2026

ADHD and brain rhythms

Scientists recorded the brain waves of adults with and without ADHD. In adults with ADHD, the waves kept the same pattern for longer. The study shows the two go together. It does not show that one causes the other.

Haque et al., Translational Psychiatry (2026). "ADHD symptoms across the population are explained by individual variability in brain criticality."
