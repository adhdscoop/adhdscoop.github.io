ADHD after 50 and dementia risk

Scientists used health records from 187,341 US adults aged 50 and older. About 7 in 100 adults with ADHD later had dementia or milder memory problems. For adults without ADHD, it was about 3 in 100. Most adults with ADHD never did. The study shows the two go together. It does not show that one causes the other.

Bauer-Negrini et al., JAMA Psychiatry (2026). "Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder and Late-Life Cognitive Impairment."

November 12, 2026

3 brain patterns in kids with ADHD and anxiety

Scientists looked at brain scans from 3,508 kids with ADHD, anxiety, or both. They found three brain patterns. Each pattern came with its own mix of symptoms. The patterns are a research finding. They are not a test anyone can take.

Nature Mental Health (2026). "Deep learning of brain-behavior dimensions identifies transdiagnostic biotypes in youth with ADHD and anxiety disorders."

November 12, 2026

ADHD and brain rhythms

Scientists recorded the brain waves of adults with and without ADHD. In adults with ADHD, the waves kept the same pattern for longer. The study shows the two go together. It does not show that one causes the other.

Haque et al., Translational Psychiatry (2026). "ADHD symptoms across the population are explained by individual variability in brain criticality."
